## Learning Tools
- **Pinyin Converter**: Convert any Chinese text into pinyin with accurate tone marks. Paste a paragraph or a single character and instantly see the pronunciation — https://www.example.com/en/tools/pinyin-converter/
- **Pinyin Chart**: Complete interactive pinyin table with 23 initials and 24 finals. Click any cell to hear pronunciation, switch between tones, and learn phonetics rules — https://www.example.com/en/tools/pinyin-chart/
- **Stroke Order Practice**: Learn to write Chinese characters with the correct stroke order. Animated guides show every stroke so beginners build proper habits from day one — https://www.example.com/en/tools/stroke-order/
- **HSK Flashcards**: Practice HSK 1 through HSK 6 vocabulary with spaced repetition. Track which words you know and focus your review where it matters most — https://www.example.com/en/tools/hsk-flashcards/
- **Tone Trainer**: Master the four Mandarin tones through listening and comparison drills. Train your ear to distinguish ma, ma, ma and ma with confidence — https://www.example.com/en/tools/tone-trainer/
- **Picture Learning**: Learn Chinese vocabulary through pictures. Three difficulty levels from everyday nouns to four-character idioms, with audio pronunciation — https://www.example.com/en/tools/picture-learning/
- **Chinese Reading Reader**: Graded reading passages with on-demand pinyin annotation and tap-to-translate vocabulary. Read at your level and grow your understanding naturally — https://www.example.com/en/tools/reading-reader/
- **Radical Explorer**: Decompose any character into its radicals and components. Discover the meaning behind each building block and remember characters faster — https://www.example.com/en/tools/radical-explorer/

## HSK Learning Path
- HSK 1 (150 words): Beginner - greetings, numbers, basic phrases
- HSK 2 (300 words): Elementary - daily conversations and simple exchanges
- HSK 3 (600 words): Pre-Intermediate - travel, shopping, routine topics
- HSK 4 (1,200 words): Intermediate - work, study, and opinion exchange
- HSK 5 (2,500 words): Upper-Intermediate - news, essays, and abstract ideas
- HSK 6 (5,000+ words): Advanced - fluent reading and professional use
