## HSK Learning Path
- HSK 1 (150 words): Beginner - greetings, numbers, basic phrases
- HSK 2 (300 words): Elementary - daily conversations
- HSK 3 (600 words): Intermediate - basic reading and writing
- HSK 4 (1200 words): Upper-Intermediate - fluent daily communication
- HSK 5 (2500 words): Advanced - reading newspapers, watching movies
- HSK 6 (5000+ words): Proficient - near-native fluency

## About
SkillXM provides free Chinese learning tools for the global community. All tools run in the browser, require no registration, and are completely free. The site also offers a Chinese version (https://www.skillxm.cn/) for native Chinese speakers with elementary education tools.
